When planning travel during winter months, a primary consideration is whether spending more for a legacy carrier is worth the cost compared to selecting a budget airline, given the risks of weather-related cancellations. Cancellation rates can significantly vary among the months and regions, especially during the winter season, when weather conditions can be severe. The 2024-2025 winter season presented a weak La Niña event, which the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration’s (NOAA) “U.S. Winter Outlook: Warmer and drier South, wetter North,” predicted that the northern tier would experience wetter-than-average conditions, while the southern tier would experience drier-than-average conditions [4]. This is relevant to our selected airports, as Boston Logan and Chicago O’Hare fall in the northern tier, while Dallas-Fort Worth falls in the southern tier. These contrasting weather predictions by region provide a foundation to analyze how weather patterns can affect airline operations across varying geographic locations.
